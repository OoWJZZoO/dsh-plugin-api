## Introduction

`session-activity-projection` 为第三方插件提供统一、可重建、可去重的 session activity projection：把一次真实的 session turn / execution 从 agent、LLM、tool、approval、question 与 session durable 事实中收敛为冻结活动视图。公共主面是 `projection`，公共路径 `pluginApi.sessions.activity`（host）。

**实现通道方向（v2）：facade B 投影 + R 能力切片。**

- 投影 owner（facade）负责 identity、evidence model、查询/订阅、重放去重、availability；
- R 能力切片落在既有 `dsh-agent-loop` replacement owner 包（`plugin-api-agent-loop`）上，与 `session-interaction-operation` 共用同一切片的 attempt 生命周期事实契约（同组件同包第二/三 feature 先例：U19 assembled-context evidence）。该切片在 loop 的真实提交点发出 attempt 事实（开始/终结/outcome/队列后续状态），使 `aborted`、`superseded`、`success`、`error` 与"排队 vs 空闲"在切片激活时是 **observed** 证据，而不是 reconstruction 猜测；
- 选择性安装未包含该切片、或版本错配导致切片停用时，投影按 availability 如实降级：缺失事实回到 reconstruction/unknown，绝不把猜测标成 observed（诚实降级 ≠ 逃避能力——full 安装默认交付全保真，见 Requirement 9）。

本 feature 使用 M9 契约 §2 冻结词汇，不重新定义；`session-interaction-operation` 用本投影的词汇表达 request/execution 关联；`client-attention-contribution` 以本投影为事实来源；`checkpoint-restore-contract` 引用本投影的 identity/终态词汇与 live-attempt 证据。冻结基线内交付（`0.1.0-rc.6-0.1.0`，`dsh.api: 0.1`，不步进任何版本字段）。每条需求标注 A/B/C/R 分类与适用约束。

## Requirement 1: Session-Scoped Activity Identity And Correlation

**User Story:** As a plugin author, I want each real session activity to carry one stable identity and an explicit correlation to the underlying execution, so that I can track the same activity across events, replay and reconnect without guessing.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a new activity is first observed for a session THEN the system SHALL create an activity record whose identity `activityId` is generated by the facade projection owner, is unique within the facade lifetime, and SHALL NOT reuse an event sequence number, cursor, or official private execution field as the activity identity; after a view rebuild, `activityId`/`seq`/`cursor` SHALL be deterministically regenerated from the same durable evidence (same evidence source ⇒ same record within the facade lifetime), and continuity across facade lifetimes SHALL NOT be implied.
2. GIVEN the loop boundary slice is active WHEN slice attempt facts carry an `executionId`/`attemptId` THEN the projection SHALL correlate the activity with those identifiers.
2a. GIVEN the slice is inactive WHEN official events or durable records evidence a correlation THEN the projection SHALL produce it, and SHALL otherwise leave correlation `unknown`.
3. WHEN an activity record is created THEN it SHALL carry the owning `sessionId`, and SHALL NOT be visible under another session's scope.
4. WHEN multiple official sources (agent-loop events, slice attempt facts, durable session records, tool/approval records) refer to what is evidently the same activity THEN the system SHALL correlate them into one activity record and SHALL NOT create duplicate records for the same evident activity.
5. WHEN an evidence source becomes available after the activity was first observed THEN the correlation fields SHALL be updated only from new evidence and SHALL be frozen in the public snapshot with their evidence source recorded.
6. WHEN evidence is ambiguous about whether two observation groups belong to the same activity THEN the system SHALL keep them as distinct records with explicit `unknown`/`unavailable` correlation markers and SHALL NOT merge them heuristically.

**Classification:** A/B facade projection over official events/durable records plus R slice attempt facts (`dsh-agent-loop` owner 包共享切片) as the observed correlation source; identity generation follows `identity-and-lifecycle.md` §1.

## Requirement 2: Evidence Model — Observed, Reconstructed, Unknown, Unavailable

**User Story:** As a plugin author, I want the projection to tell me how much of the activity view is directly observed versus inferred, so that I never mistake a guessed state for a proven one.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the projection publishes an activity fact THEN every status, terminal or correlation field SHALL carry an evidence classification drawn from `observed | reconstructed | unknown | unavailable`, where `observed` requires a real official event, durable record, or slice attempt fact; `reconstructed` requires a documented deterministic derivation; `unknown` means evidence is missing or ambiguous; `unavailable` means the required source is not reachable in this installation.
2. WHEN a session shows no terminal evidence for a period of silence THEN the system SHALL NOT report the activity as succeeded; silence SHALL be `unknown` or an explicit waiting/interim state with its evidence.
3. WHEN the projection claims that a model saw a content segment, an agent executed a step, or an approval decision was produced THEN the claim SHALL be backed by the corresponding real assembled/send, step/tool, or approval evidence; content provenance SHALL only expose its provable part.
4. WHEN two classifications could apply to the same field THEN the system SHALL prefer the stronger truthful classification and SHALL record the derivation rule; the snapshot SHALL NOT collapse `unknown`, `unavailable`, `degraded` or `partial` into each other or into success.
5. WHEN the projection is queried THEN each snapshot SHALL carry `observedAt` and, where applicable, `updatedCursor`.

**Classification:** B facade semantics + R slice facts as observed inputs; classification vocabulary unchanged by the channel.

## Requirement 3: Activity State And Terminal Vocabulary With Slice-Gated Fidelity

**User Story:** As a plugin author, I want running, waiting, finished, failed, aborted and superseded to be distinguishable with the highest fidelity my installation can honestly provide.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an activity is in progress THEN its snapshot SHALL expose an interim `status` from the closed vocabulary (`preparing | running | waiting | interrupted`) with waiting kinds (`approval | question | user-message | tool | queued`) per evidence, and SHALL NOT use lifecycle words (`settled`, `committed`, `closed`, `disposed`) as status values.
2. WHEN an activity reaches a definitive end THEN its snapshot SHALL expose `terminal` from `success | error | aborted | denied | superseded`; a terminal SHALL be final and unique for that activity record and SHALL NOT be rewritten by later signals.
3. WHEN the loop boundary slice is active THEN the projection SHALL classify `aborted`, `superseded`, `success`, `error` and `denied` as `observed` from slice attempt facts (the loop commits these at its own decision points; `denied` may also be observed via the approval/decided durable joiner when correlation permits), and SHALL classify the post-attempt queue state (`queued` next work vs idle) as `observed` from the slice's attempt facts; a post-attempt `followUp: queued` SHALL be recorded as an observed queue fact on the terminal record plus a session-level current-queue marker, distinct from the in-progress `waiting.kind=queued` of AC1, and SHALL NOT rewrite the terminal back to a waiting state; the projection SHALL NOT override a slice-observed outcome with a heuristic.
4. WHEN the slice is inactive THEN the projection SHALL produce those same states (including `denied`) only as `reconstructed`/`unknown` under the documented derivation rules (newer durable user/turn boundary without a prior end ⇒ candidate `superseded`, approval-rejection durable with correlation ⇒ candidate `denied`, etc.) and SHALL surface the fidelity loss in availability and in the snapshot; SHALL NOT mark heuristic results `observed`.
5. WHEN a timeout is evidenced THEN it SHALL be `terminal: error` with reason/classification marking the timeout, SHALL NOT introduce a new terminal word.
6. WHEN the same evidence could support both `aborted` and `superseded` for one uncommitted window THEN the system SHALL apply the shared adjudication priority (`aborted` > `superseded` > `error` > timeout-`error`) once and freeze the resulting terminal.

**Classification:** R for slice-observed terminal/queue facts (`dsh-agent-loop` owner 包共享切片)；B reconstruction rules as the degraded fallback；vocabulary per `identity-and-lifecycle.md` §3 and contract §2.3.

## Requirement 4: Frozen Projection Queries

**User Story:** As a plugin author, I want stable query verbs that return frozen, redacted activity views, so that I can render or decide without mutating anything.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a caller queries the current activity THEN `pluginApi.sessions.activity.current(sessionId)` SHALL return a frozen snapshot or a typed absence result; SHALL have no side effects and SHALL NOT leak writable state.
2. WHEN a caller queries a specific activity THEN `get(activityId)` SHALL return the frozen record with full evidence classification; unknown records SHALL return typed absence/unavailable, not an exception.
3. WHEN a caller lists activities THEN `list({ sessionId, cursor?, limit? })` SHALL return a frozen ordered page by the projection's stable `seq`/cursor semantics and SHALL support cursor continuation.
4. WHEN a caller requests history THEN `history(sessionId)` SHALL return frozen past records bounded by durable evidence and SHALL state truncation/unavailability in the view.
5. WHEN a member cannot be served THEN it SHALL return the typed unavailable/degraded result per the availability contract and SHALL NOT throw through the caller except P1 (`PluginApiInactiveError`) / P2 (`PluginApiFeatureDisabledError`).
6. WHEN any query returns content THEN redaction SHALL be applied host-side by audience before freezing; secret/owner-private material SHALL NOT appear in snapshots unless an explicit visibility policy lifted it.

**Classification:** A/B projection (`api-idioms.md` §3.1; host-only in v1 — client activity projection is an explicit non-goal recorded in the deviation notes).

## Requirement 5: Observe Subscriptions

**User Story:** As a plugin author, I want one `observe` idiom that notifies me of activity changes and replays the current view.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a caller subscribes THEN `observe({ sessionId })` SHALL return `{ current(), subscribe(listener), dispose(), epoch }`; `current()` SHALL return the frozen current snapshot at call time.
2. WHEN the activity changes THEN each listener SHALL receive the frozen new snapshot or typed change record; listener exceptions SHALL be contained.
3. WHEN a handle is disposed THEN it SHALL stop invoking listeners, SHALL be idempotent, and a stale disposer SHALL NOT remove or affect another observer or a rebuilt epoch.
4. WHEN an observer rebuilds after reconnect or epoch rollover THEN the new handle SHALL expose a new `epoch`; old-epoch callbacks SHALL NOT be delivered on the new handle and SHALL NOT mutate the current view.
5. WHEN multiple plugins observe the same session THEN subscriptions SHALL be additive and independent; registration order SHALL NOT encode business semantics.
6. WHEN delivery ordering matters THEN the handle SHALL deliver changes in the projection's stable seq order; business order beyond that is the caller's responsibility (`ordering.md` §1).

**Classification:** A/B projection observer; shape per `api-idioms.md` §3.1 and the delivered durable-observer precedent.

## Requirement 6: Durable Replay, Cursor Dedupe And Reconnect

**User Story:** As a plugin author, I want activity views to survive reconnect and event loss without double counting.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the projection owner rebuilds a view after reconnect, host reload or epoch rebuild THEN it SHALL replay or join bounded durable session evidence using a cursor, SHALL dedupe records already incorporated, and SHALL NOT double-apply an event, durable record or terminal.
2. WHEN slice attempt facts are not durable and the host reloads THEN rebuilt views SHALL NOT claim `observed` fidelity for facts that only the lost slice events evidenced; those fields SHALL fall back to `reconstructed`/`unknown` with the fidelity note until new attempt facts arrive.
3. WHEN replay cannot reach all evidence THEN the rebuilt view SHALL mark each affected fact with exactly one of `unknown` or `unavailable` (`unavailable` when the source is not reachable in this installation, `unknown` when evidence is missing or ambiguous), SHALL surface the marker per-view, and SHALL NOT collapse the two or present a partial view as complete.
4. WHEN a late event or durable record arrives for an activity that already committed a terminal THEN the system SHALL accept it only as redacted diagnostic/audit material and SHALL NOT rewrite the view, start a new attempt, or present it as the current activity's result.
5. WHEN duplicate durable records of the same fact are observed THEN the system SHALL apply the fact once per activity and cursor and SHALL record dedupe provenance bounded to diagnostics.
6. WHEN an event sequence is not contiguous THEN the projection SHALL expose a gap marker and SHALL NOT guess missing facts as success.

**Classification:** B projection semantics; fidelity rules follow `concurrency-and-cancellation.md` §4 and contract §2.2 (`cursor`/`epoch` 不冒充 identity).

## Requirement 7: Stale Result And Generation Containment

**User Story:** As a plugin author, I want late callbacks from old owners or old epochs to be harmless.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an asynchronous result, callback or disposer belongs to an older owner context, generation or observer epoch THEN the projection SHALL check commit eligibility (owner, generation, epoch, terminal state) before any state update or listener delivery.
2. WHEN eligibility fails THEN the result SHALL NOT update the current view, SHALL NOT be delivered as the current activity's change, SHALL NOT clean up a newer observer or another owner's resources, and MAY be kept as bounded diagnostic/audit material.
3. WHEN the projection owner itself rebuilds (epoch rollover) THEN old-epoch internal workers SHALL be closed and SHALL NOT write into the new epoch's state.
4. WHEN an official event source emits a payload that predates the current cursor THEN the projection SHALL ignore or quarantine it per the dedupe contract and SHALL NOT reorder the view backwards.

**Classification:** B facade containment; follows `concurrency-and-cancellation.md` §4-§5.

## Requirement 8: Execution And Attempt Correlation

**User Story:** As a plugin author, I want retries and new triggers distinguishable, so that one logical operation never looks like several unrelated ones.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN internal retry or attempt escalation occurs within the loop THEN the slice attempt facts SHALL expose it as the same execution identity with a new attempt; attempts SHALL NOT create new activity identities.
2. WHEN an external caller re-triggers the same action THEN that SHALL be a new execution correlation with its own activity record when separately evidenced, and SHALL record parent/cause correlation when available.
3. WHEN an activity is superseded by a newer external trigger THEN the older record SHALL expose its supersession terminal per Requirement 3 and SHALL NOT continue receiving new attempt evidence.
4. WHEN the slice is inactive or the official loop provides no identity THEN the projection SHALL use the facade execution identity when produced or SHALL leave correlation `unknown`, and SHALL NOT fabricate an identity from event sequences.

**Classification:** R for slice-carried attempt/execution correlation; B facade fallback; rules per `identity-and-lifecycle.md` §1 and `durable-state-and-scope.md` §3.

## Requirement 9: Loop Boundary Slice Contract (R, 共享切片)

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want the loop-side facts slice to preserve the official agent-loop contract completely while adding attempt lifecycle facts, so that observed fidelity never breaks the loop it observes.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the slice is added to the agent-loop replacement package THEN it SHALL extend the already-replaced `agent-loop` official row through the existing replacement package and patch mechanism only; it SHALL NOT modify official package files and SHALL NOT add, disable or re-insert rows.
2. WHEN the slice is active THEN the replacement SHALL reproduce every ctx service, event, timing, payload, receiver, error and disposer contract of the replaced official row before adding the attempt-fact interface; additions SHALL be additive on that full contract (cap-strategy R2).
3. WHEN the slice emits attempt facts THEN they SHALL be registered canonical events with frozen payloads and semantics `fact`, with producer authority owned by the agent-loop owner slice, and SHALL NOT alter official event timing or payloads: `agent/attempt/start` SHALL carry at least `attemptId`, `operationId` (when the attempt belongs to an external request operation), `executionId` (when available), `sessionId`, `seq`, `observedAt`; `agent/attempt/end` SHALL carry at least `attemptId`, `sessionId`, `outcome` (five-terminal vocabulary `success|error|aborted|denied|superseded`), `reason`/`classification` (redacted), `followUp` (`none`|`queued`), `seq`, `observedAt`.
4. WHEN the slice applies THEN it SHALL verify the official row is disabled, exactly one replacement row is active, runtime and package identities match (`A.B.C` and locked runtime), the facade's internal observation contract is compatible, and no component-owner conflict exists (cap-strategy R4/R5/R6).
5. WHEN a self-check fails THEN the slice SHALL log bounded diagnostics and stay inactive; the official-contract behavior of the replacement SHALL remain functional and the projection SHALL report the fidelity loss per Requirement 3 AC4 — the installation SHALL NEVER be left with the official row disabled and no working official-contract path (cap-strategy R4/R6).
6. WHEN third-party code directly imports the official `@deepseek-ai/dsh-agent-loop` package THEN the slice SHALL NOT claim to intercept or replace that import surface (cap-strategy R3).
7. WHEN the client-half determination is recorded THEN it SHALL document each of the capability-strategy §10 six questions with evidence and conclude host-only (the replaced official row declares no client manifest and exposes no client namespace/slot/settings/host-client negotiation/browser state/client event).
8. WHEN the slice is delivered THEN it SHALL register its U-series upstream proposal and retirement condition in feature-list §3.1 at the integration wave (capability-strategy §4.1).
9. WHEN the official component later provides an equivalent public attempt/settle seam THEN the slice SHALL follow the registered retirement condition to allow migration back to official binding.
10. WHEN the slice and `session-interaction-operation`'s slice areas are both delivered by the same agent-loop owner package THEN their contracts SHALL be registered as one shared package capability in feature-list §3.1, with the attempt-fact vocabulary defined once and consumed by this feature, `session-interaction-operation` and `checkpoint-restore-contract`.

**Classification:** R（既有 owner 包内扩展）；capability-strategy R1–R8 适用条款逐条满足并测试。

## Requirement 10: Availability And Degradation

**User Story:** As a plugin author, I want to know exactly what fidelity my installation can serve, so that partial capability never masquerades as full truth.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a caller queries `sessions.activity.availability()` THEN the system SHALL return frozen `{ status: active | degraded | unavailable, reason?: string }`, SHALL never throw, and SHALL NOT mix health information into availability.
2. WHEN the loop boundary slice is inactive or version-mismatched THEN the projection SHALL remain active at reduced fidelity: observed-grade terminal/queue facts are unavailable, and availability SHALL report `degraded` with the concrete reason (e.g. `terminal-evidence=reconstructed`) while official-event/durable-sourced facts stay served.
3. WHEN an official evidence source is missing in the installation THEN the projection SHALL stay active for the sources it can serve and SHALL report the missing source in `availability().reason` and in per-view markers.
4. WHEN the projection owner fails to initialize THEN it SHALL log bounded diagnostics, expose `degraded`/`unavailable`, and SHALL NOT throw through apply or boot.
5. WHEN the facade core is inactive or the capability disabled THEN callers SHALL receive uniform P1/P2 errors per contract §6.
6. WHEN capability presence is negotiated THEN `capabilities` SHALL expose the activity capability and its fidelity-relevant slice capability without package or row identities.

**Classification:** Facade selfDescription; taxonomy per contract §6 and `capability-strategy.md` §6.2 (选择性安装/错配只停用相关 slice，不波及其他能力)。

## Requirement 11: Feature Boundaries — No Action Authority

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want the projection to stay read-only, so that it never becomes a hidden scheduler, retry loop, notification sender or session mutator.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a plugin needs to request, cancel, retry or notify THEN the projection SHALL NOT expose those actions; they belong to `sessions.request`/`cancel`, recovery/approval authorities and attention contribution.
2. WHEN the projection consumes official events or slice facts THEN it SHALL NOT dispatch, transform or veto any canonical event; it SHALL only subscribe as an observer.
3. WHEN the projection observes content or provenance THEN it SHALL NOT write into session durable history, SHALL NOT alter prompts or model context, and SHALL NOT act as a policy decision point.
4. WHEN the projection is active THEN it SHALL NOT become the owner of executions, recovery decisions, leases or any other authority.
5. WHEN events are replayed, duplicated or lost THEN the projection SHALL NOT compensate by inventing business facts; gaps SHALL remain visible.

**Classification:** Boundary/non-goal across A/B/R; no framework dispatch semantics are moved into this feature (`capability-strategy.md` §2).

## Requirement 12: Multi-Owner Composition And Consumer Safety

**User Story:** As a plugin author, I want the activity projection to behave identically under many concurrent plugins.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN several plugins query or observe the same session THEN all SHALL receive the same frozen view semantics from the single projection owner and SHALL NOT observe each other's private state.
2. WHEN a plugin subscribes with invalid input THEN the system SHALL return the typed invalid-input/absence result and SHALL NOT corrupt other subscriptions.
3. WHEN a plugin is disposed, reloaded or its owner context ends THEN its observers SHALL be cleaned up without affecting other owners' observers or the projection state.
4. WHEN a view or handle is handed to a caller THEN it SHALL be frozen or read-only.
5. WHEN the projection runs in headless, web-host and TUI-host contexts at the same runtime identity THEN it SHALL present the same query/observe semantics for the sources available in that context, with per-context degraded reporting.

**Classification:** A/B additive/pure composition; `composition-and-authority.md` §3/§5.

## Requirement 13: Verification And Delivery Gates

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want mechanical evidence that the projection never overclaims and the slice never breaks the loop.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN delivery tests run THEN two synthetic plugins in reverse registration order SHALL prove query/observe independence, owner cleanup, duplicate subscriptions and callback containment.
2. WHEN slice tests run THEN official-contract parity, version mismatch, boot self-check, owner-conflict, no double-run, removal/official-row restoration, and slice-inactive degraded projection SHALL be covered on real or contract-faithful loop fixtures.
3. WHEN fidelity tests run THEN each documented derivation and each slice-observed fact SHALL have fixtures proving input evidence and classified output; slice-active runs SHALL assert observed-grade terminal/queue facts, slice-inactive runs SHALL assert reconstruction and `unknown` and SHALL assert overclaim (silence → success, heuristic → observed) is impossible.
4. WHEN replay/reconnect tests run THEN fixtures SHALL cover host reload (observed→reconstructed fidelity fallback per Requirement 6 AC2), late events after terminal, duplicate records, cursor gaps, epoch rollover and stale disposers.
5. WHEN redaction tests run THEN snapshots, remote payloads (where any) and logs SHALL be asserted free of secret/owner-private material.
6. WHEN registry/shape checks run THEN every new public/handle member and the new `agent/attempt/*` catalog entries SHALL be registered with one primary idiom, semantic face, effect, composition, scope, authority, event semantics and availability shape; surface snapshots SHALL match.
7. WHEN the guarded full test suite, `git diff --check`, registry/surface consistency, official-package zero-modification audit and the required global adversarial review run THEN all SHALL pass before the Stage 4 completion commit.

**Classification:** Delivery foundation covering B facade, the R slice and the degraded path.

## R 决策与残余 C 类登记（v2）

- 采纳 R：`dsh-agent-loop` owner 包共享 loop boundary slice（attempt 生命周期/队列事实）。对应能力：`aborted`/`superseded`/settle/queue 从猜测升级为 observed（Goal 六态中消费者最依赖的一对"被取代 vs 排队"获得可靠答案）。
- 未采纳 R（各附证据，均为 A 类直绑已够或 R 契约不可证明）：
  - `dsh-session`/`session-projection`：事件与 durable 记录已全量公开（A）；本投影不写 durable 记录，无记录 owner 缺失。
  - `dsh-llm`：turn 终结由 durable 边界承载；stream settlement 不需要替换 llm 行。
  - `dsh-tools`/approval：`tools/result`、durable `tool/call|result`、`approval/asked|decided` 已公开（A）。
- 残余 C（证据 + 退役条件）：官方 durable per-request identity 与 question 事实 seam——官方无公开面，非单一组件可闭合约缺失；官方提供后投影直绑并删除对应 reconstruction 规则。执行中（in-flight）状态不属于本投影范围（in-flight = 未提交事实的 attempt/tool/provider 内部上下文；投影的 interim status 由已提交事实派生，与 in-flight 不同轴）。

## M9 Contract Conformance And Deviation Notes

- M9 契约 §2 词汇逐条采纳；§5 动词采纳（`current/get/list/history/observe`）。
- 偏离记录（契约 §8）：
  1. **host-only v1**（同 v1）：原始 activity 的 client projection 面不交付，Web/TUI 客户端呈现经 `client-attention-contribution` 派生；补偿测试同前。
  2. **共享切片契约**：attempt 事实词汇与 `session-interaction-operation` 定义同一切片（§3.1 联合登记），三个消费 feature 不各建词汇；补偿：共享词汇机械一致性测试。
  3. **诚实降级语义**：full 安装交付 observed 保真；选择性安装/错配时降级为 reconstruction 并在 availability 明示——这是安装模式语义（`versioning-and-protocols.md` §6），不是能力逃避；补偿：双态 fidelity 测试（Requirement 13 AC3）。
